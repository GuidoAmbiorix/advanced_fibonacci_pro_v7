**Key Variables**:
- `GV_PORTFOLIO_RISK` - Current total exposure (must be ≤2.0)
- `GV_PORTFOLIO_SYMBOLS` - Number of open positions
- `GV_COOLDOWN_EURUSD` - Cooldown expiry time after consecutive losses

- ... (one per symbol)

**If GV_PORTFOLIO_RISK stuck at 2.0**:
- Too many positions open
- Governor blocking new trades
- Wait for one to close or manually close a position
